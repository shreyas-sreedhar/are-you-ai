Staying safe

Nobody real asks for gift cards

No bank, government office or company will ever ask you to pay with gift cards. If someone does, it is a scam every time.

Look the number up yourself

If a message says it is from your bank, do not use the number it gives you. Find the number on your card or your statement.

Urgency is the trick

Rushing you is how scams work. A real problem will still be there in an hour, after you have talked to someone you trust.

Asking is not a bother

Show anything that worries you to a family member or a friend. People who care about you would far rather be asked.
